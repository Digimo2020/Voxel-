Screenshot Guide (v1.0) - 2026-10-30

Use genuine captures from the currently running Voxel application only.

Recommended individual images:
- selection-control.png — the floating two-action selection control
- mini-player-playing.png — compact player while speech is playing
- settings-voice-controls.png — voice, speed and expression settings
- document-reader.png — document reading controls
- system-tray.png — app menu in the Windows notification area or macOS menu bar

Capture guidance:
- Use a normal desktop resolution and a readable UI scale.
- Do not use mockups, generated interfaces, composite panels, or outdated screenshots as if they were current.
- Do not show personal documents, email addresses, usernames, notification contents or other private information.
- Keep separate images as separate files.
- Record animations as a short GIF or MP4 from the real application; do not simulate app behaviour with a fabricated animation.
