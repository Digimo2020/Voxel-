# Voxel Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

Voxel is a free, open-source text-to-speech app by Digimo.

**What Voxel does with your data**
- **Selected or pasted text** is sent to Microsoft's online neural speech service (Edge TTS) to create audio. If you are offline, Voxel uses the speech engine built into your computer instead, and nothing is sent.
- **Clipboard:** to read a selection Voxel briefly copies it, then restores what was on your clipboard.
- **Mouse and keyboard events:** Voxel listens for the end of a text selection (mouse release and shortcuts). It does not record, store or send what you type.
- **Settings, history and recordings** stay on your computer in your user folder.

**What Voxel does not do**
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry.
- Voxel does not sell or share your data.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/Digimo2020/Voxel-/issues

Microsoft's handling of text sent to its speech service is covered by Microsoft's own privacy statement.
